📊 What drives Airbnb prices in Athens?
I analyzed Airbnb listings in Athens to identify which factors have the biggest impact on pricing.
Key Findings
🏆 1. Location is the strongest pricing factor (Importance: 0.200)
 The neighborhood has the biggest influence on listing prices. A property in central, high-demand areas such as Zappeion operates in a completely different price range compared to listings in outer suburbs.
🛏️ 2. Number of bedrooms matters (0.106)
 More bedrooms are strongly associated with higher prices, making property size one of the most important value drivers.
🚿 3. Number of bathrooms is almost equally important (0.105)
 Bathrooms appear to signal both space and comfort, contributing significantly to pricing power.
🏠 4. Room type has a major impact (0.083)
 Whether a listing is a private room or an entire property plays an important role in determining its market value.
👥 5. Guest capacity influences price (0.049)
 The number of guests a property can accommodate affects pricing, although less than physical space characteristics.
Surprising Insights
⭐ Review scores have limited pricing influence (0.019)
 Higher guest ratings do not appear to translate into significantly higher prices in the Athens market.
🌟 Superhost status has minimal impact (0.017)
 While it may improve trust and booking conversion, the Superhost badge alone does not command a substantial price premium.
📍 Latitude still matters
 Even after accounting for neighborhood differences, a property's north-south position within Athens continues to influence pricing, suggesting additional location effects beyond neighborhood boundaries.
Conclusion
The Athens Airbnb market is primarily driven by location and property characteristics, not host reputation metrics. If hosts want to maximize pricing potential, focusing on where the property is located, bedroom count, bathroom quality, and overall space offering appears far more impactful than chasing marginal gains from ratings or badges.
